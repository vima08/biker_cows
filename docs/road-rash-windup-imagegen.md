# Road Rash — right-hand windup art and atlas packing

Tool: built-in imagegen. Date: 2026-10-07.

Final project assets:

- `public/assets/road-rash/road-rash-heroines-atlas-v1.png`
- `public/assets/road-rash/road-rash-riders-atlas-v3.png`

The original whole sprites were isolated and packed into 3072×1024 transparent atlases: six columns of 512 pixels, row heights 341/341/342. Pivot X=256 in each cell; rear tyre contact is the bottom pixel. The windup art changes only heroine frames [Cassia, Bruna]/1 and all three rival frames/1. The other 31 isolated frames were copied byte-for-byte before PNG encoding. Nova's already right-handed windup is retained.

`scripts/bake-road-rash-atlases.mjs <heroine-windup.png> <rival-windup.png>` performs the packing. The source-frame pixel scale is retained; padded cells accommodate extended weapons. Runtime `riderAtlas.ts` reads these canonical cells directly, without segmentation or RGB removal. The legacy preparation path remains available for raw source sheets.

## Heroines prompt

In each second-column sprite the raised weapon arm currently appears on the LEFT of the image; redraw this anticipation pose so the armed hand is raised on the RIGHT SIDE of the image, matching the immediately following third-column sprite's extended right-arm attack. This must be a redraw of the arm and shoulder, NOT a horizontal flip of the entire rider or motorcycle. Keep the motorcycle, rear wheel, front wheel, exhaust, head, hair and torso facing the exact same original rear three-quarter camera as their first and third-column sprites. No mirrored bike. Neutral first-column and extended third-column sprites are the orientation references. In each windup the OTHER hand stays holding the handlebar on the left; the RIGHT armed hand raises the existing weapon upward at the right shoulder, ready to swing right. Keep the entire raised weapon strictly within that second-column sprite's grid rectangle, especially the third-row boss weapon MUST NOT extend above its row. Exact existing1536x1024 canvas, regular6columns x3rows. Same detailed hard-outlined arcade sprite style, exact same identity, costume, motorcycle and palette in every row. Actual transparent alpha background. No scene, checkerboard, text, labels or grid. Input image1 is the exact HEROINES atlas: row1 Cassia short chestnut hair, golden-brown fur, red scarf, black/gold outfit and red/gold bike; row2 Bruna black mane, dark fur, cobalt armor and blue bike; row3 Nova white quiff, amber visor, magenta scarf, black/crimson suit, cream/red bike. Row3 Nova's anticipation arm is already on the RIGHT; keep this row3 second-column sprite unchanged, and redraw only row1/row2 windups. Keep all six Nova sprites and her nearly straight-rear camera unchanged.
